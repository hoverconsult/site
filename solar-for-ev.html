One vehicle. Different energy answers.

Do not size the system from the battery badge alone.

An EV moves part of your energy spending from the fuel station to the electricity meter. The design question is not simply how large the car battery is. It is how much you drive, when the vehicle is parked, how quickly energy must be replaced and what the rest of the property already consumes.

Two homes with the same vehicle can need completely different systems. Hover Consult therefore starts with behaviour and operating requirements before selecting equipment.
