Solar load offloading in Ghana: move selected loads, not the whole building

Partial solar can deliver useful resilience and consumption reduction without designing for every appliance in the building.

Hover Consult Technical Team25 July 20268 minute read

Solar load offloading means transferring a defined group of loads to a solar or hybrid supply while other loads remain connected to the grid or another source. It can be a practical response where full-property solar is unnecessary, unaffordable or constrained by roof area.

Choose the circuits deliberately

Typical candidates include lighting, routers, televisions, security, computers, refrigeration and selected sockets. Electric cookers, water heaters, large air-conditioners and heavy machinery may stay outside the solar-backed distribution unless specifically engineered.

Separate wiring is essential

A qualified electrician should identify circuits, confirm phases, create an essential-load board where appropriate and prevent unsafe back-feeding. Labels and a clear single-line diagram help occupants and technicians understand the arrangement.

Ghana planning note: Load offloading is a circuit-design strategy, not simply plugging appliances into a different socket.

Match solar production to operating hours

Daytime business loads can often consume solar energy as it is produced, reducing storage requirements. Overnight or outage loads require enough usable battery energy and a charging plan.

Plan behaviour and changeover

Occupants need to know which sockets are backed up, what not to connect and what happens when battery limits are reached. The system should fail predictably rather than invite improvised extension leads.

Power the important part well before attempting to power everything poorly.

Important: This educational article is not a site-specific electrical design, financial valuation or guarantee of system performance. Final decisions require a load assessment, site inspection and appropriately qualified professionals.
