What to prepare before a power consultation

Good preparation turns a general conversation into a focused technical decision.

Hover Consult Technical Team22 July 20265 minute read

You do not need to become an electrical engineer before a consultation. You only need reliable information about what happens at your property and what outcome matters most.

Prepare six inputs

  1. Appliance list: names, quantities and rating-label photos where possible.
  2. Usage pattern: normal working hours and which appliances run together.
  3. Outage experience: frequency, duration and the activities affected.
  4. Priority: what must remain on and what can be scheduled.
  5. Existing system: model labels, age, symptoms and previous repairs.
  6. Future plan: likely expansion, new equipment or property changes.

Bring documents and images

Supplier quotations, utility bills, roof or equipment-area photos and distribution-board images can reduce uncertainty. Do not send passwords, payment credentials or sensitive personal documents.

A consultation is most valuable when the problem, constraints and desired result are clear.

Know what the session should produce

Agree whether you need a diagnosis, quotation review, preliminary architecture, site-assessment recommendation or installation support plan. A consultation is not automatically a final engineering design or binding quotation.
