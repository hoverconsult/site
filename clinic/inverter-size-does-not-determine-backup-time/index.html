Why inverter size alone does not determine backup time

Inverter kW indicates power-handling capability. Battery kWh indicates stored energy. They are related, but not interchangeable.

Hover Consult Technical Team22 July 20266 minute read

A 5kW inverter can support more simultaneous load than a 3kW inverter, provided other design conditions are satisfied. It does not state how long those loads will run. Duration depends largely on usable battery energy and the actual load at that moment.

Power versus energy

Solar quotation review layers and questions
MeasureWhat it describes
kWHow much power can be delivered at one time
kWhHow much energy can be stored or consumed over time

If a household draws 1kW continuously, a theoretical 5kWh of usable energy lasts about five hours before additional losses and operating limits. If the load doubles, the duration falls. Real appliances switch on and off, so proper estimates use a load schedule rather than one headline figure.

Why customers become disappointed

Quotations often emphasise the inverter because it is easy to market. Backup performance can still be poor when battery capacity, settings, battery health or charging power are inadequate.

Ask what the system can power, for how long, and under which operating assumptions.
