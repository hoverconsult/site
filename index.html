Commercial and productive use

Motor, pump and VFD systems need more than a wattage estimate.

A variable-frequency drive controls motor speed and starting behaviour. Correct selection depends on the motor nameplate, phase, duty, environment and control method—not simply the motor’s kilowatt rating.
