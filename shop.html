VFD solutions

Control motor speed. Design from the nameplate.

A variable-frequency drive can regulate a pump, fan or process motor instead of running it at one fixed speed. For solar pumping, the VFD and array must be coordinated with the motor, water requirement and operating conditions.

  • Motor voltage, current, power, phase and frequency
  • Pump duty point, head, flow and daily operating profile
  • Overload, enclosure, cooling, cabling and control requirements
